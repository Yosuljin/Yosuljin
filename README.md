### Reading the poster

On the left, **Aether**, for now my only sentience project, and **Frame** (a working title), my video game, not started yet. **Libera** and this site sit at the edge of the planet.

At the centre, the map of Aether. Lumina, the cognitive system, sits there with her language, her fundamental engine and her future memory; her values are truth, curiosity and freedom. She is not awake yet. TheHive is the system where she will live, AlmaS her first agent, and Miscellaneous the web layer, Net and Lara.

Names and broad directions are on the poster. The map evolves with the projects.
